How To Play Pen Fight

Place pens face-to-face. You have to flick them to drop your opponent's pen and keep your pen.

Some Rules Of Pen Fight

  1. Gel Pens Are Forbidden Because it is hard to Drop
  2. If flick your pen yourself, and no pen left
    you/your team, you'll be lose
  3. You must flick.
  4. If someone touch/flick the pen but it is not his move, It will be a Penalty.
    Penalty is Dropping it from above while holding it in the palm of the hand.
  5. If Someone move twice, Opponent will get a penalty
